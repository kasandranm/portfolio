2023 Harris Half-year Acquisition Recap

Previous Post
Next Post

After a great close to 2022, we are thrilled that Harris has started off the first half of 2023 with great momentum. Thanks to the exceptional dedication of our global teams, Harris completed 13 acquisitions spanning seven industries. Four of those acquisitions are in verticals outside of our core businesses, adding new growth opportunities and setting the stage for an exciting future ahead.

Harris welcomed over 300 employees via our acquisitions, and they will continue to deliver best-in-class solutions to more than 5,000 customers, including retail point-of-sale platforms, customer engagement and marketing solutions for the utility sector, and child care solutions.

Check out our newest acquisitions below:

 

 

 

 

A foothold in Financial Services and Insurance

Together, QuoteBurst and AGO Insurance establish our new portfolio of financial services and insurance software businesses.

"I am extremely pleased with the initial phase of our new portfolio in Financial Services and Insurance (FS&I). We have recruited a talented team, acquired some quality businesses, and hired our first EVP for a new Business Unit.  As we move forward my goal for our team is pretty simple: to make FS&I and Harris as closely associated as we are with Utilities, Public Sector and Schools and Healthcare. It's a really tall order but exactly the audacious goal the team and I signed up for"

 

QuoteBurst specializes in strategic marketing solutions for insurance agents while AGO Insurance offers personal and commercial policy processing systems along with accounting, underwriting, claims and reporting solutions.

About Harris
Harris acquires vertical market software businesses, manages them using industry best practices, and builds them for the future. Through acquisitions, Harris has grown extensively from its roots in the utilities, local government, education, and healthcare sectors to operate over 200 businesses globally across more than twenty industries. Harris is an operating group of Constellation Software Inc. (TSX: CSU), one of North America’s most active acquirers of software businesses.
