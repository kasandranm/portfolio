Looking back on 2021 | M&A Advisor Relationships

Previous

M&A advisors advocate, educate and manage crucial dealmaking processes for their clients and Harris was honored to work with new and familiar experts in 2021. Eight of last year’s acquisitions were guided by M&A Advisors, supporting Harris’ growth in multiple sectors across the USA and France. A selection of Harris’ brokered transactions is displayed below.




When working with Harris, M&A Advisors can find assurance in our reliable processes and experienced in-house teams who are equally committed to finding the best solutions, creating optimal outcomes for sellers.



The diversity of businesses we acquired in 2021 showcases that Harris continues to actively pursue opportunities within existing or new verticals and geographies. If you have opportunities to discuss, please contact us.

About Harris

Harris acquires vertical market software businesses, manages them using industry best practices, and builds them for the future. Through acquisitions, Harris has grown extensively from its roots in the utilities, local government, education, and healthcare sectors to operate over 170 businesses globally across more than twenty industries. Harris is an operating group of Constellation Software Inc. (TSX: CSU), one of North America’s most active acquirers of software businesses.
