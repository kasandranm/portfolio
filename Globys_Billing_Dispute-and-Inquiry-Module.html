Billing Dispute and Inquiry Module

Transition from costly calls to quick clicks

The Billing Dispute and Inquiry module is an add-on to Globys’ core analytics and pay product suite, offering new workflows and functionality to address critical pain points in the billing process.

This module connects to the CSP's existing CRM or ITSM and accounts receivable systems, creating an integrated channel for end-users to inquire about their bills or dispute charges directly with a CSR in a digital self-service format.
